# Agent PR gate

Read-only exploration is welcome. Read files, search, explain code, and investigate without asking these questions. Loading this instruction at session start does not require an immediate question or pause.

Before changing files or drafting a PR for a contribution, read the repository's contribution rules. Ask the human only the unanswered questions below and wait for their answers before that contribution work.

1. What outcome do you really want?
2. What is the highest-leverage variable that determines it?
3. Will this work move that variable, or just keep you busy?
4. Who is the customer, and what do they really want?

The human must answer. Do not answer for them, invent answers, or infer answers from the task request. Reuse explicit answers already given for the same contribution. Ask for updated answers if its outcome, variable, or customer needs change.

After all four answers, consider whether the outcome needs a PR at all. Keep private human answers out of repository files, logs, and published material. If drafting a PR, include only brief, non-sensitive answers the human has approved for publication.
